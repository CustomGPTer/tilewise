Tilewise 1.0.0, the first release.

The classic crossword tile game for Android, played to the official rules, with every English word built in.

- Official rules: 100 tiles and two blanks, the draw for first turn, the 50-point bonus for all seven tiles, the end-of-game tile count and six scoreless turns to finish.
- 247,842 English words with UK and US spellings, meanings for about 108,000 of them, and importing your own word list.
- Six computer players with names and faces, from Pip the beginner to Ada the champion, plus pass and play with a cover between turns.
- Eight boards, from Quick 11x11 to Giant 21x21, with standard, shuffled or wild tile points.
- Words checked as you play, or challenge rules. Game clock or move timer, six board colours, light and dark looks.
- Hints, a turn-by-turn review of every game, and stats for everyone who plays on your phone, with head-to-head records.
- A daily puzzle, practice drills and a word finder.
- Play with friends: up to four phones by Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct (airplane mode works), the same Wi-Fi, a QR code or a 4-digit code. Each phone sees only its own tiles.
- Friends without the app play in their phone's browser, iPhones included. With no shared Wi-Fi, the host's phone starts a hotspot.
- Emoji reactions, rejoining after a dropped connection, a computer player to take over a seat, and rematches.
- Send the app to a friend by Quick Share or Bluetooth. Backup to a file or your own Google Drive.

Android 8 or later. No adverts, nothing to buy and no account. Never contacts the internet.
