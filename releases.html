Release notes

What changed in each version of Tilewise. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.1 22 November 2026Latest

  • When tiles break a rule, such as a word that doesn't join the tiles already on the board, they now turn red, the same as for a word that isn't in the word list.
  • The message at the top of the game now shows in full on two lines instead of being cut off.

Version 1.0.0 22 November 2026

  • First release. The classic crossword tile game to the official rules: 100 tiles and two blanks, the draw for first turn, the 50-point bonus, the end-of-game tile count and six scoreless turns to finish.
  • 247,842 English words with UK and US spellings, meanings for about 108,000 of them, and importing your own word list.
  • Six computer players with names and faces, from Pip the beginner to Ada the champion, plus people sharing one phone with a cover between turns.
  • Eight boards (Classic, Quick, Compact, Crossroads, Diamond, Grand, Giant and Random), with standard, shuffled or wild tile points.
  • Words checked as you play, or challenge rules with lose-a-turn or 5-point penalties. The computer players challenge too.
  • A game clock or a move timer, six board colours including high contrast, light and dark looks, and sounds and vibration you can switch off.
  • Hints with your rack's eight best plays, and a turn-by-turn review of every game with the best play and your accuracy.
  • Stats for everyone who plays on your phone: win rate, form, streaks, a score chart, points per play, best plays, bonus squares, big-tile plays and head-to-head records.
  • A daily puzzle with stars and a day streak, practice drills, and a word finder.
  • Play with friends: up to four phones by Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct (airplane mode works), the same Wi-Fi, a QR code or a 4-digit code. Each phone sees only its own tiles, and dropped phones rejoin their seat.
  • Friends without the app can play in their phone's browser, iPhones included. With no shared Wi-Fi, the host's phone starts a hotspot for them, and they can download Tilewise straight from it.
  • Emoji reactions, a computer player to take over for anyone who leaves, and a rematch with everyone still connected.
  • Send Tilewise to a friend by Quick Share or Bluetooth, and back up to a file or your own Google Drive.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.1 is called Tilewise.apk. It is 7.3 MB (7,274,308 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

16481b333f0593390f1f686007825da0cff11057bcda5a49d305f4db0c7080c2

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Tilewise.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
