What Tilewise does not do

Tilewise has no online play over the internet and no matching with strangers: the phones need to be on the same Wi-Fi or close together. It does not include the licensed tournament word lists, and it is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Scrabble GO and Words With Friends match you with players anywhere in the world, have extra game modes and events, and update themselves from Google Play.
