Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy covers the Tilewise app for Android (package uk.jj.tilewise) and this website. Tilewise is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Your games, stats and settings are kept on your phone. They are never uploaded to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Tilewise never contacts the internet. It only talks to other phones in a game you host or join, on your own Wi-Fi, its own hotspot or directly by Bluetooth.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a backup file, or the app itself shared with a friend.

What stays on your phone

Tilewise keeps these in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Your games: the board, the moves, the scores and the players' names.
  • The names and colours of the people who play on your phone, so their stats build up.
  • Daily puzzle results, practice scores and your settings.
  • If you import one, your own word list.
  • For a game you host, a code and a short random key for each other phone, so they can rejoin their seat. They are deleted when the game ends.

Android's own app backup is switched off for Tilewise, so nothing is copied to a Google account without your knowledge.

Playing with other phones

When you host or join a game, the phones send each other only what the game needs: each player's name and colour, the moves, the scores, emoji reactions and, for each phone, its own tiles. A random id for each phone lets your stats record who you played. Nothing goes through the internet or any server: phones connect over your own Wi-Fi, a hotspot started by the host's phone, or directly by Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct.

Bluetooth play uses Google Play services' Nearby Connections, which is part of Android on most phones. Google's own terms apply to Play services.

A friend who plays in a browser loads the game page from the host's phone. Their browser keeps their name, colour and a seat key in its own storage on their phone, so they can carry on if the page reloads.

Tilewise does not read your contacts, photos, microphone or messages.

Backups and sharing

A backup file holds your games, people, puzzle results and settings. It is made only when you ask, and saved where you choose, such as your own Google Drive. If you switch on keeping it up to date, the same file is rewritten after each finished game. When you share the app with a friend, Android's share sheet passes the app file to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy terms apply.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove any game from the home screen, and uninstalling Tilewise deletes everything the app holds. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the page itself. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub's servers on the same terms.

Children

Tilewise collects nothing from anyone, so it is suitable for all ages. Younger players should ask a parent before linking phones with people they don't know.
